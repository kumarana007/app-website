Privacy Policy

Your documents matter. Learn how QuickScan Lite handles your files, permissions, and advertising data.

Your documents stay on your device

QuickScan Lite is designed for local document processing. Your scanned documents and PDF files are not uploaded to QuickScan Lite servers. Advertising services may separately process device and advertising-related information.

01

Introduction

Welcome to QuickScan Lite, an offline document scanner and PDF toolbox.

This Privacy Policy explains how QuickScan Lite handles information when you use our application. By using the app, you acknowledge the data practices described in this policy.

QuickScan Lite is designed to process documents locally without requiring user registration or uploading document contents to our servers.

02

Information stored on your device

QuickScan Lite stores document-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Scanned document images and pages
  • Imported images and PDF files
  • Generated PDF documents
  • Document names and app preferences
  • Temporary files used during processing

QuickScan Lite does not upload these document contents to its own servers.

Local data can be removed using available document-deletion controls. Uninstalling the app generally removes data in the app's private storage, subject to your device's backup and storage settings. Exported files may remain outside the app.

03

Camera and file permissions

QuickScan Lite may request access to certain device features to provide scanning and document-management functions.

  • Camera: Used to capture document pages when you choose to scan.
  • Photos and files: Used to select images or PDFs for importing and processing.
  • Storage: Used to save, export, and manage documents as supported by your device.

These permissions are used for document functionality and not for uploading your personal documents to our servers. Permissions can be managed through your device settings.

04

Advertising and third-party services

QuickScan Lite uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded-interstitial ads.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information, such as:

  • Advertising identifiers and device identifiers
  • Advertising interactions and usage information
  • Device, app, and diagnostic information
  • Information used to deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertisements

Advertising-related processing is governed by applicable consent requirements and the relevant third-party privacy policies. Advertising providers may process data on servers outside your device.

For more information, please read Google's Privacy Policy .

05

Accounts, payments, and analytics

QuickScan Lite does not require users to create an account or sign in to use its document scanning features.

We do not operate a cloud account system or a remote document-processing service. QuickScan Lite does not operate its own analytics or user-profiling service.

Third-party advertising services may independently collect advertising, measurement, and diagnostic information as described in the previous section.

06

Data security

QuickScan Lite is designed to keep scanned documents and generated files on your device. Local document protection also depends on your device's security settings.

We encourage you to keep your device updated and use appropriate screen-lock and device-security features.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07

Data retention and deletion

Your locally stored documents remain available until you delete them, clear application data, or uninstall the application, depending on your device and backup settings.

You can manage or delete saved documents using the app's available controls. Files exported to other locations must be deleted separately from those locations.

Advertising providers may retain data in accordance with their own privacy and retention policies.

08

Children's privacy

QuickScan Lite is a general productivity application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal document contents through our own servers. Third-party advertising practices are subject to applicable laws and platform policies.

09

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application features, third-party services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
