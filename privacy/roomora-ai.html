Transform Your Room With AI

Roomora AI lets you create an AI-assisted redesigned view of your room from a photo.

Upload or capture a room photo, choose the type of room, select an interior design style, add your preferences, and generate an AI-assisted interior design concept.

How Roomora AI Works

  1. Add a room photo

    Upload a photo from your device or capture a new room photo using your camera.

  2. Choose your room type

    Select the type of room you want to redesign.

  3. Pick an interior design style

    Choose the design style you want for your room.

  4. Add finishing touches

    Add additional design preferences such as your preferred design options and colour palette.

  5. Generate your room redesign

    Roomora AI processes your selected room photo and design preferences to create an AI-assisted redesigned room image.

  6. View your design

    Review the generated room design and visualize how your space could look with a different interior style.

  7. Save and revisit designs

    Keep your generated designs so you can view them again.
