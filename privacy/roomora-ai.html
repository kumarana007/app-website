Privacy Policy

Welcome to Roomora AI. This Privacy Policy explains how Roomora AI handles information when you use our application.

Roomora AI allows you to upload or capture a room photograph, select a room type and design preferences, and generate an AI-assisted redesigned image.

By using Roomora AI, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Access

Photos and Images

When you choose to create a design, Roomora AI may access the room image that you select from your device or capture using the camera.

The selected image may be uploaded to our backend services so that it can be processed by our AI image-generation service and used to provide your generated room design.

Camera

If you choose to take a room photograph using the camera, Roomora AI requests camera access. Camera access is used only when you choose to use the camera functionality.

Photo Library / Media

If you choose an existing photograph from your device, Roomora AI may request access to the relevant photo or media selected by you.

Account Information

Roomora AI may process account and authentication information needed to provide your account and app functionality.

Design Preferences

Roomora AI may store design selections such as room type, design style, design mode, and colour palette so that your requested design can be generated and displayed.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Roomora AI may be used to:

  • Provide camera and photo-selection functionality.
  • Process room photographs submitted for AI redesign.
  • Generate AI-assisted room redesign images.
  • Store and display original and generated design images.
  • Store your design preferences and generation history.
  • Maintain your account and application state.
  • Manage subscription access and available AI generations.
  • Provide customer support and improve application reliability and functionality.

3. AI Image Processing

Roomora AI uses third-party AI image-generation services to transform room photographs into AI-assisted interior design concepts.

When you request a design, your selected room image and relevant design instructions may be transmitted through our backend to an AI processing provider.

The resulting generated image may then be transferred to our storage infrastructure so that it can be displayed in the application.

Important: Please do not upload photographs containing information that you do not want processed by Roomora AI or its service providers.

4. Data Storage

Roomora AI uses cloud infrastructure to store information required to operate the application.

Depending on how you use the application, stored information may include:

  • Account and authentication information.
  • Design metadata and preferences.
  • Original room photographs.
  • AI-generated room images.
  • Generation and subscription-related information.

Images and related design records are stored using access controls intended to help prevent unauthorized access.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant application features, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and enforce applicable agreements.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Roomora AI may offer paid subscription plans that provide additional features, including increased AI generation availability and an ad-free experience.

Purchases made through the Android application are processed through Google Play's billing system.

Roomora AI may use a subscription management service, such as Qonversion, to manage subscription status, entitlements, purchase validation, and related subscription information.

Roomora AI does not receive or store your complete Google Play payment credentials.

6. Advertising

The free version of Roomora AI may display advertisements.

Third-party advertising providers may collect or process certain information from your device in connection with advertising, which may include advertising identifiers, device information, and information related to ad interaction.

Advertising providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

Paid Pro subscriptions are intended to provide an ad-free experience.

7. Third-Party Services

Roomora AI may use third-party services to provide application functionality.

Depending on the current version of the application, these services may include providers for:

  • Authentication and account management.
  • Cloud database services.
  • Cloud image storage.
  • AI image generation.
  • Subscription management.
  • Advertising.
  • Analytics and application performance.
  • Crash reporting and diagnostics.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

The specific services used by Roomora AI may change as the application is updated.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled by Roomora AI.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices

  • You can choose whether to grant camera permission when requested by the application.
  • You can choose which photographs to upload or use for AI generation.
  • You can manage application permissions through your Android device settings.
  • You can stop using Roomora AI and uninstall the application from your device.
  • You may contact us regarding requests concerning your personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Roomora AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.

11. Data Deletion
