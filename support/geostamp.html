How can we help?

Welcome to the GeoStamp Support Center. If you have questions or experience a problem while using GeoStamp, we're here to help.

Common Issues

📷 Camera is not working

Make sure GeoStamp has permission to use your device camera. You can check this under your Android device's Settings → Apps → GeoStamp → Permissions.

📍 Location is incorrect

Make sure location services are enabled on your device and that GeoStamp has permission to access your location.

🖼️ Photos cannot be accessed

Check that GeoStamp has the required photo or media permission in your Android device settings.

📱 App is not working correctly

Try restarting the application and make sure you are using the latest available version of GeoStamp.
